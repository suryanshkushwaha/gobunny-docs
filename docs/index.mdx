GoBunny gives every room its own QR code. A guest scans it and opens your
branded guest portal.

There they get the Wi-Fi password, order from your menu, request services, and
browse your local recommendations. Your team sees each request in the dashboard
and in Telegram.

<SectionGrid />

## Who this guide is for

This guide is for your team: owners, managers, and front-line staff.

It is not for your guests. Guests scan the QR code in their room and the portal
opens, with no app, no account, and no sign-in. [The guest
portal](#the-guest-portal) describes what they see, so your front desk can
answer their questions.

## How to use this guide

If you are setting up GoBunny for the first time, follow
[Getting started](/getting-started). It covers setup in order, from creating
your account to printing your first room QR code.

Otherwise, find the screen you are on in the sections below. They follow the
dashboard's own structure.

<Callout title="Good to know">

Screenshots are not included. Every screen is described by
the labels you see on it, so you can follow along with the page open.

</Callout>

## Inside one property

Open a property to see its tabs across the top. Menu, Services, and Local Guide
appear once you switch those features on in Settings.

| Tab | Guide |
| --- | --- |
| Rooms | [Rooms](/properties/rooms) |
| Menu | [Menu](/properties/menu) |
| Services | [Services](/properties/services) |
| Local Guide | [Local Guide](/properties/local-guide) |
| QR Customization | [Property QR customization](/properties/property-qr-customization) |
| Branding | [Property branding](/properties/property-branding) |
| Settings | [Property settings](/properties/property-settings) |

Two things here override what you set for the whole business:

- **Menu, Services, and Local Guide** start from what you built in Master
  Setup. Choose what this property offers, set its prices, and set the order
  guests see.
- **Branding and QR Customization** start from your account-wide look. Change
  them here only where this property needs to differ.

## What you can see

GoBunny hides screens your role does not have access to. If a page in this
guide is not in your sidebar, your role does not include it.

See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-and-permissions) for what
each role can do. To get more access, ask an owner or admin.
