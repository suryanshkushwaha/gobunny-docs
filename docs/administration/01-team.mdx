Invite colleagues, decide what each of them can do, and limit them to specific
properties.

Go to **Administration → Team**.

Owners and admins can do everything here. Property managers can invite people
below their own level but cannot change existing access.

## What is on the page

| Section | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Members** | Everyone on the team, with their role and property access. |
| **Recent Invites** | Invitations sent but not yet accepted. |
| **Custom roles** | Any roles you have built yourself. |

## Invite a teammate

1. Select **Add team member**.
2. Fill in the form.
3. Select **Create invite**.
4. Select **Copy** and send the link to your colleague.

<Callout title="Good to know">

GoBunny makes the link but does not email it. Copy it and
send it yourself — email, WhatsApp, or on a note across the desk.

</Callout>

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Email** | The address they sign in with. One address belongs to one GoBunny account. |
| **Role** | Only roles below your own are offered. |
| **Property access** | Pick specific properties, or leave it empty for all of them. Not shown for Admin, which is always account-wide. |
| **Custom role name** | Only when the role is **Custom**. For example "Night supervisor". |
| **Permissions** | Only when the role is **Custom**. You can only grant what you hold yourself. |

Under the form, a preview lists exactly what the selected role can do. Read it
before you send.

### The roles

| Role | For |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Running the account without ownership transfer. Always account-wide. |
| **Property manager** | Running assigned properties end to end. |
| **Services manager** | Guest services end to end — the master catalog, what each property offers, and the requests that come in. |
| **Front desk** | Reception. Sees rooms, handles front-desk requests. |
| **F&B manager** | The kitchen and food orders. |
| **Housekeeping** | Housekeeping requests. |
| **Maintenance** | Maintenance requests. |
| **Read only** | Looking, not touching. |
| **Custom** | An exact set of permissions you choose. |

→ [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-and-permissions)

## What your colleague does next

They open the link, enter their name and a password, and join. If GoBunny asks
them to confirm their email, they do that first.

→ [Accept a team invite](/before-you-sign-in/accept-team-invite)

## Manage pending invites

| Action | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Copy invite link** | Copies it again, if the first message got lost. |
| **Revoke invite** | Cancels it. The link stops working at once. |

## Change someone's access

1. Find them under **Members**.
2. Select the edit icon to open **Edit team member access**.
3. Change the role, the custom role name, the permissions, or the property
   access.
4. Save.

Changes take effect on their next page load. You cannot promote anyone to or
above your own level.

## Remove someone

Find them under **Members**, select the remove icon, and confirm.

This deletes their sign-in for this account and everything tied to it: their
profile, their membership, their property access, and their Telegram
connection.

Any requests assigned to them are unassigned first and stay in the queue, so no
work is lost. Their past requests and audit entries stay.

You cannot remove yourself, or anyone at or above your own level.

## Custom roles

Choose **Custom**, name it, and tick the exact permissions.

Permissions are grouped: Account, Dashboard, Team, Properties, Rooms, Branding
and domains, Menu, Services, Local guide, Guest requests, and Integrations.

Two rules:

- You can only grant permissions **you** hold.
- Team management cannot be granted through a custom role.

The name you choose appears wherever that teammate's role is shown.

Use a custom role when a preset is close but not right — a night manager who
handles every request but must not change your setup, for instance.

## Property access

Most roles can be limited to specific properties. Someone limited to two
properties sees only those two: in the properties list, in the request queue,
in Telegram, in the property switcher, and in audit logs.

Leave the field empty for access to every property, including ones you add
later.

**Admin** is always account-wide.

## What else to know

- Invite links expire after seven days. Send a new one if a colleague is slow
  to join.
- Everyone needs an active membership. If one is deactivated, they can still
  sign in but your business will not load for them.
- Team and role changes are recorded in [Audit logs](/administration/audit-logs).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| A field is highlighted on the invite form | Read the message under it. |
| "Choose at least one property." | You chose limited access but picked nothing. Pick some, or clear the field for all. |
| "Name this custom role." | Custom roles need a name of at least 2 characters. |
| "Choose at least one permission." | Custom roles need at least one permission ticked. |
| "Invites unavailable" | Your role can see this page but not invite. Ask an owner or admin. |
| The email is already in use | That address already has a GoBunny account. Use another. |
| A role you want is not in the list | It is at or above your own level. Ask an owner or admin to send that invite. |
