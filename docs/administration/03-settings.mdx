Your own details, and your business details.

Go to **Administration → Settings**.

Everyone can reach this page. **Business Settings** appears only if your role
includes it — owners and admins by default.

## Profile Settings

| Row | Editable | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **First name** | Yes | Up to 80 characters. |
| **Last name** | Yes | Up to 80 characters. |
| **Email** | No | This is your sign-in and cannot be changed here. |
| **Password** | By link | Select **Reset Password** and GoBunny emails you a link. |

To edit, select **Edit** in the section header, make your change, then
**Save**.

To change your password, select **Reset Password** on the Password row, then
open the link GoBunny emails you.

→ [Reset password](/before-you-sign-in/reset-password)

## Business Settings

| Row | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Business logo** | JPG, JPEG, or PNG under 200KB. Cropped square or circle. |
| **Business name** | 2 to 120 characters. |
| **Business tagline** | Up to 160 characters. Optional. |

To edit, select **Edit** in the section header, make your changes, then
**Save**.

### Change the logo

1. In edit mode, select **Upload logo** and choose a file.
2. Drag and zoom to frame it.
3. Choose **Square** or **Circle**.
4. Save the crop, then **Save** the section.

**Adjust logo** reopens the crop for the current image, so you can change the
shape or framing without uploading again.

## Where these appear

| Setting | Shows up in |
| --- | --- |
| Business logo | The sidebar, the guest portal, and the centre of your QR codes if that option is on. |
| Logo shape | Everywhere the logo appears. |
| Business name | The sidebar, the dashboard heading, and QR preview cards. |
| Business tagline | Under the dashboard heading. |
| Your name | Request assignments and audit log entries. |

<Callout title="Good to know">

Changing your logo here updates every QR preview,
download, and print card on its own. You do not need to touch your QR
settings.

</Callout>

Your own profile and your business details are separate permissions, so a
manager can change their own name without being able to change your business
identity.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Logo must be smaller than 200KB." | Compress or resize the image. |
| "Logo must be a JPG, JPEG, or PNG file." | Convert the file. SVG and WebP are not accepted. |
| **Business Settings** is missing | Your role does not include it. Ask an owner or admin. |
| No password reset email | Check spam, then try again from the Password row. |
| Your email is wrong | It cannot be changed. Ask an owner or admin to invite the correct address and remove the old one. |
