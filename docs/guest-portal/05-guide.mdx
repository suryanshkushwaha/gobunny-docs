Your local recommendations, with one-tap directions.

Needs **Local Guide** switched on in
[Property settings](/properties/property-settings).

## The list

A header reads **Local Guide** — "Hand-picked places nearby, saved by your
hosts."

Below it are the guides you switched on for this property, grouped by category
in this order:

Food & Drink, Places to Visit, Shopping, Essentials, Activities, Nightlife,
Other.

Categories with nothing switched on are hidden.

## A card

| Element | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Image | Across the top, if you gave a photo address. |
| Name | The place. |
| Description | Why it is worth going. |
| **Tip:** | Your insider tip, highlighted. |
| **Navigate** | Opens your Google Maps link. |

**Navigate** opens the exact link you saved. On a phone that usually opens
Google Maps with the place already selected, ready for directions.

<Callout title="Good to know">

This button is the whole point of the tab. Check each link
lands on the exact venue, not a general area — a guest standing on a street
corner will not forgive a pin two blocks off.

</Callout>

## What guests see

Only the guides switched on for this property, in the order you set on its
Local Guide tab.

→ [Local Guide](/properties/local-guide)

## Empty and closed states

| Situation | The guest sees |
| --- | --- |
| The guide feature is off | "Closed — The local guide is closed for the time being." |
| Feature on, nothing switched on | "No recommendations yet — Nearby recommendations are currently unavailable." |
