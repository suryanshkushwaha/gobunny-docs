Where guests track what they have asked for.

This is the tab that stops them ringing reception to ask whether the order is
coming.

## The list

A header reads **Request Status** — "Track the live status of your orders and
service requests for Room \{number\}."

Each request below is a card.

| Element | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Icon | A fork for a food order, a bell for a service request. |
| Type | **DINING ORDER** or **SERVICE REQUEST**. |
| Time | When they sent it. |
| Status | See below. |

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Received, not started. |
| **In Progress** | Your team is on it. |
| **Completed** | Done. |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled, by the guest or by your team. |

A food order shows each item with its quantity and line total, whether it is
immediate or scheduled, and the total. A service request shows the service
name, its category, and the price if there is one.

Both show the guest's own note, if they left one.

## Live updates

The status updates about every 20 seconds while the guest has the page open. If
they lock their phone or switch apps it pauses, and picks up again when they
come back.

When something changes, they get a sound and a message:

| Change | Message |
| --- | --- |
| → In Progress | "Your \{order/request\} for \{summary\} is now in progress." |
| → Completed | "Your \{order/request\} for \{summary\} is complete!" |
| → Cancelled | "Your \{order/request\} for \{summary\} has been cancelled. Please contact the front desk." |

<Callout title="Good to know">

Marking a request **In Progress** is worth the click. The
guest gets told someone picked it up, which is usually what they wanted to
know.

</Callout>

## Cancelling

A guest can cancel only when all three are true:

1. You allow cancellation for that kind of request in
   [Property settings](/properties/property-settings)
2. The request is still **Pending**
3. The window is still open

They see the exact deadline. While the window is open, it reads
**"Cancellation allowed until 07:30 PM"** with an active **Cancel Request**
button. Once it passes, it reads **"Cancellation window was closed at 07:30
PM"** and the button greys out.

Selecting it asks "Cancel this request? This action cannot be undone."

| Request | Deadline |
| --- | --- |
| Immediate | When they sent it, plus your "cancel within" minutes |
| Scheduled | The delivery time they chose, minus your "cancel before" minutes |

Both use the property's time zone.

Guest cancellations show in your queue and in
[Audit logs](/administration/audit-logs) marked **Guest portal**, so
you can tell them from your team's.

## Before they ask for anything

> **No requests yet** — "Your orders and service requests will appear here in
> real-time."

## Why a guest might see an empty list

The list lives on the guest's own phone, because they never sign in. So:

- Clearing browser data empties it. The requests are untouched in your
  dashboard.
- Scanning from a different phone gives an empty list.
- Private or incognito mode loses the list when the tab closes.
- One room's portal can never show another room's requests.

If a guest loses their list, your team can still see everything in
[Guest requests](/dashboard/guest-requests) and tell them where it
stands.
