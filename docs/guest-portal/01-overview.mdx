What your guests see when they scan the QR code in their room.

It is a mobile web page. Nothing to download, no account, no sign-in.

These pages describe the guest side so your team can answer questions and check
the portal looks the way you meant it to.

## How a guest gets there

They scan the card in their room, and their phone opens the portal for **that
room**. That is all.

The address is your GoBunny address — or your own, if you connected one — plus
the room's code:

```
https://stay.yourhotel.com/r/RM-1234-ABCD
```

## On every screen

### The banner

- Your cover image, slightly darkened
- Your logo as a badge in the top left
- Your welcome line and the property name
- A light/dark toggle in the top right, if you allow dark mode

Without a cover image, or if the image fails to load, your background colour is
used. Guests never see a broken image.

### The room card

Just below the banner: the room name, a green **Checked-in** marker, and
**Checkout at \{time\}** — the room's checkout time, or the property default.

### The tabs

A floating bar with five.

| Tab | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Wi-Fi, shortcuts, and a call button. |
| **Dining** | Menus and ordering. |
| **Services** | Requests to your team. |
| **Guide** | Your local recommendations. |
| **Status** | What they have asked for, and where it is up to. |

## Features you have switched off

Switch a feature off in
[Property settings](/properties/property-settings) and its tab stays in
place, showing a **Closed** notice. Its card on Home dims and is marked
**(Closed)**.

<Callout title="Good to know">

Nothing disappears and nothing looks broken. Guests simply
see that it is not available, which is what you want when the kitchen closes
at ten.

</Callout>

## The back button works

Tab changes go into the phone's history, so pressing back steps through the
tabs instead of throwing the guest out of the portal.

## Privacy

Guests never sign in and never give you their name, email, or phone number.

Their status list lives on their own phone. In practice:

- A guest who clears their browser data loses the list. The requests themselves
  are untouched in your dashboard.
- A guest who scans on a different phone starts with an empty list.
- One room's portal can never show another room's requests.

If a guest cannot see a request they made, ask them to check the phone they
made it on. You can always find it in your queue.

## If a guest sees "Room Not Found"

The scanned code does not match a room. Usually:

- The room was deleted
- The property was deleted
- The code was printed against a web address you have since removed
- The card is damaged, or printed too small to scan

Check the room still exists in [Rooms](/properties/rooms), then reprint
the card.
