Where guests land after scanning.

The first thing a guest wants is the Wi-Fi password, so it is at the top.

## Wi-Fi

The first card shows the **Network** name, the **Password**, and a full-width
**Copy Wi-Fi Password** button.

Tapping it copies the password and confirms with "Wi-Fi password copied. Paste
it into your network settings." The button briefly reads **Copied Password**.

GoBunny uses the room's Wi-Fi override if it has one, and the property default
otherwise. If neither a name nor a password is set, the whole card is hidden.

## Shortcuts

Under a **Guest Services** heading, three cards:

| Card | Opens | Reads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Local Guide** | Guide | "Explore nearby food, sights, and essentials" |
| **Concierge** | Services | "Request towels, laundry, or room service" |
| **Menu** | Dining | "Order food from the convenience of your room" |

A card for a feature you switched off is dimmed, marked **(Closed)**, and
cannot be tapped.

The **Menu** card is also closed when the property has no active menu, even if
the feature itself is on.

## Need Help?

At the bottom, a card reading **Need Help?** with a **Call Desk** button.

It dials the property's **Contact Phone**.

<Callout title="Good to know">

This is the guest's escape hatch when the portal cannot
help them. Use a number someone actually answers. If no contact phone is set,
the button is hidden entirely.

</Callout>

## Light and dark

If you allow dark mode, a toggle sits in the top right of the banner. The
guest's choice is remembered on their phone, starting from whatever their
device prefers.

Turn dark mode off in [Branding](/platform-setup/branding) and the
toggle disappears — guests always see your light theme.
