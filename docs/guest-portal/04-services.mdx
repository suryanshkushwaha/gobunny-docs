Where guests ask your team for something — housekeeping, maintenance, laundry,
the front desk.

Needs **Guest Services** switched on in
[Property settings](/properties/property-settings).

## The list

A header reads **Concierge Services** — "Select a service to send a request
directly to hotel staff."

Below it, each service you switched on is a card.

| Element | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Icon | Set by the category — sparkles for Housekeeping, a wrench for Maintenance, a shirt for Laundry, a bell for Front Desk, a compass for Other. |
| Name | What you called it. |
| Description | Your supporting line. |
| Price | Only shown if there is one, in the property's currency. |
| **Request** | Opens the request sheet. |

Only services switched on for this property appear.

→ [Services](/properties/services)

## Making a request

Tapping **Request** opens a sheet with:

- **Request \{service name\}**
- A line confirming the room
- **Charge: \{amount\}**, if the service is paid
- **Add instructions (optional)** — up to 500 characters
- The cancellation window, if you allow it
- **Submit Request**

The confirmation names the service and the room: "Extra Towels was requested
for 101." The request then joins their [Status](/guest-portal/status) tab.

## What happens on your side

The moment they submit:

1. The request appears in
   [Guest requests](/dashboard/guest-requests) with a sound and a
   pop-up.
2. The count beside **Guest Requests** goes up.
3. Telegram messages go to every connected teammate whose role covers that
   category at that property.

Routing follows the category.

| Category | Goes to |
| --- | --- |
| Housekeeping, Laundry | Housekeeping staff |
| Maintenance | Maintenance staff |
| Front Desk, Other | Front desk staff |

Owners, admins, property managers, and services managers see everything.

→ [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-and-permissions)

<Callout title="Good to know">

A request always carries your current name, category, and
price for that service — even if the guest left the page open for an hour
before sending it.

</Callout>

## Cancellation

If you allow it, the guest sees the window before submitting: "Within 5 mins of
placement".

They can then cancel from the [Status](/guest-portal/status) tab within that window.
Once your team starts the work, they cannot.

→ [Property settings](/properties/property-settings)

## Empty and closed states

| Situation | The guest sees |
| --- | --- |
| The services feature is off | "Closed — Concierge services are closed for the time being." |
| Feature on, nothing switched on | "No services active — Concierge services are currently closed." |
