Where guests browse your menus and order.

Needs **F&B Menu** switched on in
[Property settings](/properties/property-settings), and at least one
active menu on the property's [Menu](/properties/menu) tab.

## Menu status

The menu appears at the top with its name, description, hours, and a badge.

| Badge | Meaning | Can they order? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open now** | Inside the serving window. | Yes, as soon as possible. |
| **Preorder** | Closed now, opening later. Shows when — "Opens tomorrow at 7:00 AM". | Yes, scheduled for the next opening. |
| **Closed** | Nothing opening soon. | No. |

Times use the property's own time zone.

With more than one active menu, a row of chips lets the guest tap between them.
Open menus come first.

## Browsing

Items sit under your category headings, each with its name, price,
description, tags, and photo if you added one.

Two kinds of tag share the row. Highlight tags come first, in your brand
colour — **Must Try**, **Chef's Special**, **New**, **Seasonal**, **Today's
Special**. The dietary marks follow: **Veg**, **Vegan**, **Spicy**.

Highlight tags come from your master catalog unless that property overrides
them.

→ [Master menus](/master-setup/menus) · [Menu](/properties/menu)

| State | The guest sees |
| --- | --- |
| Available | An **Add** button, or **Preorder** if the menu is in preorder. |
| Out of stock | A greyed **Sold Out** label. |
| Menu closed | A greyed **Closed** label. |

Change stock from the property's [Menu](/properties/menu) tab and it
updates immediately.

## The basket

Tapping **Add** starts the basket. The item becomes a quantity stepper.

A bar appears above the tabs with the item count, the running total, and a
**View Basket** button.

<Callout title="Good to know">

A basket belongs to one menu and one delivery time. If a
guest tries to add from another menu they see "Finish or clear your current
cart before ordering another menu." That stops the kitchen receiving one
order split across two services.

</Callout>

## Checkout

**View Basket** opens **Review Order**, showing the room and the delivery
timing.

| Element | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Item list | Each item, its price, and a stepper. |
| **Cooking instructions / Special requests** | A note, up to 500 characters — "no onions, dressing on the side". |
| **Delivery time** | **Now** or **Time**. Preorder menus are fixed to a scheduled time. |
| Cancellation policy | Shown if you allow guests to cancel. |
| **Place Order** | Shows the total. Reads **Place Preorder** for a scheduled menu. |

**Now** means as soon as possible, and is only offered while the menu is open.
**Time** lets the guest pick — starting at the next quarter hour, or the menu's
next opening for a preorder.

If you allow cancellation, the guest sees the window before ordering. Immediate
orders read "Within 5 mins of placement". Scheduled ones read "Up to 120 mins
before your chosen delivery time".

→ [Property settings](/properties/property-settings)

<Callout title="Good to know">

A guest who sends several orders in quick succession is asked to pause. **Place
Order** greys out and counts down — "Please wait 90s" — above the message
"You've sent several requests just now. Please wait a moment before sending
another." It clears itself.
→ [Guest portal overview](/guest-portal/overview)

</Callout>

## Confirmation

After ordering, the guest gets **Order Placed!**, a receipt with each item and
line total, their special request, and the total paid. They close it with
**Perfect!**.

The order lands on their [Status](/guest-portal/status) tab, and in your
[Guest requests](/dashboard/guest-requests) queue straight away, with a
sound and a pop-up.

## Every order is priced from your live menu

An order that reaches your queue always carries your current names, prices, and
totals.

A guest cannot change a price, order a sold-out item, or order from a closed
menu, even if they left the page open for an hour before ordering.

## Empty and closed states

| Situation | The guest sees |
| --- | --- |
| The menu feature is off | "Closed — Dining is closed for the time being." |
| Feature on, no active menu | "No menu active — Dining orders are currently closed." |
| The menu has no items | "Menu is empty — No dining categories or items are available yet." |
