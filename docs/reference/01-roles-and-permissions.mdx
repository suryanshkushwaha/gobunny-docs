Who can see what, and who can do what.

Use this when you are deciding which role to give someone. Roles are assigned
in [Team](/administration/team).

## Two things control access

| Setting | Decides |
| --- | --- |
| **Role** | Which pages and actions someone gets. |
| **Property access** | Which properties they can work in. Leave it open for all, or pick specific ones. |

Both apply together. A property manager limited to one hotel gets full property
manager powers, but only at that hotel.

Anything a role does not include simply does not appear in their sidebar.

## The ladder

You can only invite people **below** your own level.

```
Owner
  └─ Admin
       └─ Property manager
            └─ Services manager
                 └─ Front desk · F&B manager · Housekeeping · Maintenance
                      └─ Read only
```

**Custom** roles sit outside the ladder. Anyone who can invite can build one, at
any level. What you are allowed to put inside it depends on your own role — see
[Custom roles](#custom-roles).

## The roles

| Role | For |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Whoever created the account. Full control, including ownership. Cannot be assigned. |
| **Admin** | A trusted second-in-command. Everything except ownership transfer. Always account-wide. |
| **Property manager** | Runs one or more properties end to end, including the master catalog. |
| **Services manager** | Guest services end to end — the master catalog, what each property offers, and the requests that come in. |
| **Front desk** | Reception. Sees rooms and handles front-desk requests. |
| **F&B manager** | The kitchen and food orders. |
| **Housekeeping** | Housekeeping and laundry requests. |
| **Maintenance** | Maintenance requests. |
| **Read only** | Can look, cannot touch. Good for auditors and trainees. |
| **Custom** | An exact set of permissions, under a name you choose. |

## Which requests each role sees

Requests are routed by type and category, so each team only gets its own work.

| Role | Sees |
| --- | --- |
| Owner, Admin, Property manager | Everything |
| Services manager | All service requests. Not food orders. |
| F&B manager | Food and drink orders. Not service requests. |
| Housekeeping | Housekeeping and Laundry |
| Maintenance | Maintenance |
| Front desk | Front Desk, Other, and anything uncategorised |
| Read only | Everything, with no buttons |

The same routing applies in the dashboard **and** in Telegram, with one
exception: read-only users get no Telegram notifications at all.

Property access narrows it further in both places.

## What each role can do to a request

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Owner, Admin | Update status, assign to teammates, complete, cancel. |
| Property manager | The same, at their properties. |
| Services manager | The same, for service requests at their properties. |
| Front desk | Update status and claim service requests they can see. Cannot assign or cancel. |
| F&B manager | Update status on orders they can see. |
| Housekeeping | Update status and claim service requests they can see. Cannot assign or cancel. |
| Maintenance | Update status and claim service requests they can see. Cannot assign or cancel. |
| Read only | Look only. |

### Assign versus claim

Two different models, kept apart on purpose.

- **Assign** — a manager routes work to a named teammate. Assigning does not
  change the status.
- **Claim** — a staff member takes unassigned work for themselves, which also
  moves it to In Progress. If two people claim at the same moment, exactly one
  wins.

Managers assign. Front-line staff claim.

## Property access

Most roles can be limited to specific properties. That limit applies
everywhere:

- The properties list and the property switcher
- The request queue and its counts
- Telegram notifications
- Audit logs
- Every action they attempt

**Admin** is always account-wide and has no property access setting.

## Custom roles

When no preset fits, choose **Custom**:

1. Name the role — "Night supervisor", say.
2. Tick the exact permissions from the grouped list: Account, Dashboard, Team,
   Properties, Rooms, Branding and domains, Menu, Services, Local guide, Guest
   requests, and Integrations.

You can grant the permissions your own role holds. **Claim open service
requests** is the one addition — owners, admins, and property managers can
grant it even though their own roles do not use it, so they can build
front-line roles.

Team permissions are limited by who you are:

| Your role | Team permissions you can grant |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | **Invite lower roles** and **Manage team access** |
| Admin | **Invite lower roles** only |
| Anyone else | Neither |

Request routing follows the permissions you tick. A custom role with menu
permissions receives food orders; one with full service management receives all
service requests.

The name you chose appears wherever that teammate's role is shown.

## Common setups

| Situation | Roles |
| --- | --- |
| Small hotel, owner plus two staff | Owner + two **Front desk** |
| Hotel with a restaurant | Owner + **F&B manager** + **Front desk** + **Housekeeping** |
| Group of properties, one manager each | Owner + a **Property manager** per property, each limited to theirs |
| Bringing in an accountant or consultant | **Read only** |
| A night manager who handles everything but must not change your setup | **Custom**, with request and view permissions only |

## How access is applied

- A role controls what someone can do, not only what they can see. Reaching a
  page by its address does not grant the actions on it.
- Access changes take effect on that person's next page load.
- Deactivating someone's membership means they can still sign in, but your
  business will not load for them.
