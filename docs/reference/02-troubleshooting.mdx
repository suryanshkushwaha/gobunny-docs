Common problems and the fastest way through them.

## Signing in

**You cannot sign in.**
Check the email for typos, then use **Forgot password?**.
→ [Reset password](/before-you-sign-in/reset-password)

**A colleague invited you, but sign-up says your email is taken.**
That address already has a GoBunny account. One address belongs to one account.
Ask for the invite to go to a different one.

**You sign in but your business does not load.**
Your membership may be inactive or removed. Ask an owner or admin.

**A page is missing from your sidebar.**
Your role does not include it.
→ [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-and-permissions)

## QR codes and the guest portal

**A guest sees "Room Not Found".**
The code does not match a room. Check, in this order:

1. The room still exists in [Rooms](/properties/rooms)
2. The property was not deleted
3. The code was not printed against a web address you have since removed
4. The print is clean enough to scan

**Will printed codes break if you edit a room?**
No. Room codes never change. Rename a room, change its Wi-Fi, or restyle your
QR codes and every printed card keeps working.

**What does break a printed code?**
Deleting the room, deleting the property, or removing a web address the code
was printed against.

**A code will not scan from paper.**
Usually too much styling. Try the **High Contrast Print** preset, set recovery
to **H**, shrink or remove the centre logo, and print larger. Always test one
card before a batch.
→ [QR customization](/platform-setup/qr-customization)

**Printing does nothing.**
Allow pop-ups for GoBunny, then select **Print Card** again.

**Can a guest see another room?**
No. Each code opens only its own room.

**A guest sees "Guest portal paused".**
That property or that room is paused. Resume the property from its **Settings**
tab, or the room from **⋯** → **Resume** on the Rooms tab. The printed card
starts working again immediately.
→ [Property settings](/properties/property-settings#pause-the-property)

**A property says "Paused by plan" and you did not pause it.**
More properties are active than your plan allows, so GoBunny paused the extras
instead of deleting them. Pause a property you need less and resume this one, or
move to a plan that allows more. Nothing has been lost either way.

**Your portal says "Powered by GoBunny" and you want it gone.**
That line shows throughout your trial, and on the Starter plan afterwards. It is
not a setting — Pro and Growth remove it once the trial is over. Ask an owner.
→ [Guest portal overview](/guest-portal/overview)

## Menus

**A menu shows as closed when it should be open.**
Check, in this order:

1. **F&B Menu** is on in
   [Property settings](/properties/property-settings)
2. The menu itself is switched on
3. The property's **Local Time Zone** is right
4. The menu's hours and days cover right now

The time zone is the usual culprit.

**A guest cannot order even though the menu is open.**
The item is probably marked unavailable. Check it on the property's
[Menu](/properties/menu) tab.

**A guest says they cannot order from two menus.**
Correct, and deliberate. A basket belongs to one menu and one delivery time.
They finish or empty the first, then start the second.

**A master price change did not reach a property.**
That property has set its own **Property price** for the item. Clear that field
on the property's Menu tab to go back to the catalog price.

**You cannot add a category to a property's menu.**
The menu is linked to a master menu, so its structure comes from there. Change
it in [Master menus](/master-setup/menus), or start from a template
instead of linking.

**A highlight tag you set in the catalog is not showing at one property.**
That item has **Custom tags for this property** switched on, so the property's
own choice wins — including an empty one, which shows no tags. Open the item on
that property's [Menu](/properties/menu) tab and switch it off to follow the
catalog again.

**A "Today's Special" tag is still there the next day.**
Highlight tags are not scheduled. They stay until someone removes them, so the
dated ones belong to whoever opens the kitchen.
→ [Master menus](/master-setup/menus)

## Services and guides

**Guests see no services.**
Either nothing is switched on for that property, or **Guest Services** is off
in [Property settings](/properties/property-settings).

**A new service you added is not on a property.**
New services arrive on every property switched off. Turn it on for the ones
that offer it.
→ [Property services](/properties/services)

**Something you turned off came back on its own.**
It had a scheduled return time. Turn it off again and choose to bring it back
manually.

**The wrong team is getting a request.**
Its category is wrong. Change it in
[Master services](/master-setup/services).

**A Google Maps link is rejected.**
GoBunny accepts Google Maps addresses only. Use **Share** in Google Maps and
copy that link.

## Guest requests

**Requests are not reaching your queue.**
Check your role receives that kind of request, and that you can reach that
property.
→ [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-and-permissions)

**No notification sound.**
Your browser may block audio until you interact with the page. Click anywhere
in the dashboard once, then wait for the next request.

**You cancelled by mistake.**
There is an eight-second **Undo** on the card. After that, anyone who can update
request status can move it back to pending from the **⋯** menu — every role
except read only.

**Can you delete a request?**
There is no delete button. Requests are closed by changing their status, so
your queue history stays whole. Deleting the room or the property they belong
to does remove them.

**A guest says they cancelled but you made it anyway.**
Check [Audit logs](/administration/audit-logs), filtered to Guest
Requests and Status Changes. The source tells you whether it came from the
guest, the dashboard, or Telegram, and when.

**Why can a guest not cancel?**
Cancellation is off for that kind of request, the window has closed, or your
team already started the work. Only pending requests can be cancelled by a
guest.

**A guest cannot see a request they made.**
Their status list lives on their own phone. Ask which phone they used. You can
always find the request in your queue.

**A guest says the order button reads "Please wait".**
They sent several requests in a row and reached the room's allowance — five
back to back, then one every ninety seconds. It clears itself; the button
counts down and comes back. Cancelling is never limited, and anything they
already sent is in your queue.
→ [Guest portal overview](/guest-portal/overview)

## Telegram

**Connect Telegram is greyed out.**
Telegram is not switched on for your business. Ask an owner to arrange it with
GoBunny.

**You connected but get nothing.**
Check the card says **Connected**, that your role receives that kind of
request, and that you can reach that property. Read-only users never get
Telegram notifications.

**No buttons on your messages.**
Your setting is **Updates only**. Switch to **Updates and action buttons**.
→ [Integrations](/platform-setup/integrations)

## Your web address

**It has said "Waiting" for hours.**
DNS changes take their own time. Confirm the records were added at the provider
your nameservers actually point at, then select **Check** again.

**A record says "Replace".**
Your provider has a conflicting record. Delete the old one and add the
replacement GoBunny shows.
→ [Domain](/platform-setup/domain)

**Can you use your main domain?**
No. Use a subdomain such as `stay.yourhotel.com`. Your main domain almost
certainly hosts your website.

**Can the dashboard run on your address?**
No. Your address serves guest room portals only. Sign-in and the dashboard stay
on the main GoBunny address.

## Branding

**Your changes are not showing for guests.**
Did you select **Save**? An amber **Unsaved changes** marker means you did not.

**One property looks different from the rest.**
It has a branding override on. Open its **Branding** tab and either adjust it
or turn the override off.
→ [Property branding](/properties/property-branding)

**The cover image is not appearing.**
The address must point straight at an image file. If it fails to load, GoBunny
falls back to your background colour rather than showing a broken image.

**You changed your logo but the QR codes show the old one.**
They should not — QR codes read your current logo. Refresh the page. Cards you
already printed keep the old logo, of course.

## Team

**You cannot invite the role you want.**
You can only invite roles below your own. Ask an owner or admin.

**You cannot remove someone.**
You cannot remove yourself, or anyone at or above your own level.

**What happens to work assigned to someone you remove?**
Their requests are unassigned and stay in the queue. Nothing is lost, and their
history stays.

## Data and history

**Is anything deleted for good?**
Yes, and more than you might expect. Deleting a property removes its rooms,
menus, and selections — **and its whole guest request history and audit trail**.
Deleting a room breaks its printed QR code and removes that room's past
requests. Neither can be undone.

Export or record anything you need for accounts before you delete a property.

**What is kept?**
Guest request history and audit logs survive even when the menu item, service,
or teammate is gone.

**Are Wi-Fi passwords in the logs?**
No. They are hidden before anything is written.

## Still stuck?

Note what you were doing, which property and room were involved, and the exact
message you saw.

If it concerns a specific request, the entry in
[Audit logs](/administration/audit-logs) usually has the answer.
