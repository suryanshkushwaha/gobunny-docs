Create your account, set up a property, and print your first room QR code.

## Quick start

1. Create your account and confirm your email.
2. Add a property and its rooms.
3. Turn on the guest features you want.
4. Build your master catalog, then choose what each property offers.
5. Style the guest portal and your QR codes.
6. Check the portal on your phone, then print the room codes.
7. Invite your team and connect Telegram.

Setup takes about 20 to 30 minutes for your first property.

## What GoBunny does

Every room has its own QR code. A guest scans it and opens your guest portal, a
mobile page branded as your business.

In the portal, guests can:

- Copy the Wi-Fi password
- Order from your food and beverage menu
- Request services such as housekeeping, laundry, or maintenance
- Browse your local recommendations with one-tap directions
- Track the status of everything they asked for

Requests appear in your dashboard with a sound and a pop-up alert, and in
Telegram if you connect it.

## 1. Create your account

Enter your name, your business details, and a password. You can upload your
logo now or add it later.

→ [Sign up](/before-you-sign-in/sign-up)

## 2. Confirm your email

If GoBunny asks you to confirm your email, it shows a verification screen with
links to Gmail, Outlook, and Yahoo. Open the email and select the link inside
it.

→ [Sign up](/before-you-sign-in/sign-up#email-verification)

## 3. Add your first property

A property is a hotel, homestay, resort, villa, hostel, apartment, or
guesthouse.

Set its contact details, currency, time zone, default Wi-Fi, and checkout time.

→ [Properties](/properties/properties)

## 4. Add your rooms

Add every room that needs a QR code. Each room gets a unique code in the format
`RM-XXXX-XXXX`.

To give a room different Wi-Fi or a different checkout time, open
**Different Wi-Fi or checkout?** on that room. Most rooms need neither.

→ [Rooms](/properties/rooms)

## 5. Turn on guest features

Open the property's **Settings** tab. Switch on **F&B Menu**, **Guest
Services**, and **Local Guide** as needed.

Settings is also where you choose whether guests can cancel their own orders
and requests.

→ [Property settings](/properties/property-settings)

## 6. Build your master catalog

**Master Setup** holds the services, menus, and local recommendations for your
whole business. Every property chooses from what you build here.

| Page | What to add |
| --- | --- |
| Master Setup → Services | Seven services are created for you, covering housekeeping, maintenance, and front desk. Edit them and add your own. Every property receives them switched off, ready for you to turn on. |
| Master Setup → Menus | A catalog of individual dishes and drinks, arranged into reusable templates such as "Dinner Menu" or "Beverage List". |
| Master Setup → Local Guides | Nearby places, each with a Google Maps link so guests can navigate in one tap. |

→ [Master services](/master-setup/services) ·
[Master menus](/master-setup/menus) ·
[Master local guides](/master-setup/local-guides)

## 7. Choose what each property offers

Open the property. Each tab draws from your master catalog:

- **Services** — choose the services this property offers, set its prices, and
  set the order guests see.
- **Menu** — link a master menu so it stays in step, take an editable copy of
  one, or start an empty menu.
- **Local Guide** — choose the guides this property offers, and their order.

To switch one item on or off across every property at once, use the **Property
availability** tab on the Master Setup Services or Local Guides page.

→ [Services](/properties/services) · [Menu](/properties/menu) ·
[Local Guide](/properties/local-guide)

## 8. Style the guest portal

Go to **Platform Setup → Branding**. Choose a theme preset, set your fonts and
corner style, write your welcome headline, and add a cover photo. A phone
preview updates as you edit.

Branding applies to every property. To give one property a different look,
override it on that property's **Branding** tab.

→ [Branding](/platform-setup/branding) ·
[Property branding](/properties/property-branding)

## 9. Style your QR codes

Go to **Platform Setup → QR Customization**. Choose a scan-safe preset, or open
the advanced controls for dot shapes, corner styles, colors, and a logo in the
centre.

To give one property different codes, override it on that property's **QR
Customization** tab.

→ [QR customization](/platform-setup/qr-customization) ·
[Property QR customization](/properties/property-qr-customization)

## 10. Connect your own web address (optional)

Room QR codes can point at your own address, such as `stay.yourhotel.com`,
instead of the standard GoBunny link. Add the DNS records at your domain
provider. GoBunny confirms when the address is live.

<Callout title="Good to know">

Connect your address before you print any QR codes. Codes
printed beforehand point at the old address.

</Callout>

→ [Domain](/platform-setup/domain)

## 11. Check what your guests will see

Open one room's QR code and scan it with your own phone. You see exactly what
your guest sees.

Check that the Wi-Fi password works, the menu is right, and only the features
you meant to switch on are showing.

→ [Guest portal overview](/guest-portal/overview)

## 12. Print the room QR codes

Open the property's **Rooms** tab. For each room you can view its QR code,
download it as a PNG, or print a desk tent card that includes the Wi-Fi
details.

<Callout title="Good to know">

A printed code points at a fixed address. Deleting the room
or the property, or removing a connected web address, stops that code
working.

</Callout>

→ [Rooms](/properties/rooms#qr-codes)

## 13. Invite your team

Go to **Administration → Team**. Send an invite link, choose a role, and limit
the person to specific properties if you want. To allow an exact set of actions
and nothing else, create a custom role.

<Callout title="Good to know">

GoBunny does not email invites. Copy each link and send it
yourself.

</Callout>

→ [Team](/administration/team) ·
[Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-and-permissions)

## 14. Connect Telegram

Each person connects their own Telegram account and receives guest request
notifications. Notifications can include buttons to claim, start, complete, or
cancel work without opening the dashboard.

<Callout title="Good to know">

You cannot connect Telegram on a teammate's behalf.

</Callout>

→ [Integrations](/platform-setup/integrations)

## 15. Watch the requests come in

The **Guest Requests** page is your live queue. New requests appear with a
sound and a pop-up alert, and a count appears beside Guest Requests in the
sidebar.

→ [Guest requests](/dashboard/guest-requests)

## Search

Press `Ctrl + K`, or `⌘ K` on a Mac, to open search from anywhere in the
dashboard. Start typing to jump to a property, a section inside a property, or
a settings page.
