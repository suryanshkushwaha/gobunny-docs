A one-screen summary of what is happening across your properties.

Select your business logo and name at the top of the sidebar. There is no
**Dashboard** link in the sidebar — the logo is the way back to this page.

Roles without access to this page — front desk, housekeeping, maintenance, F&B
manager, read only — land on [Guest Requests](/dashboard/guest-requests) instead.

## What you see

Your business name and tagline sit at the top. If work is waiting, a pulsing
bell appears on the right with the count.

Below that are four numbers.

| Card | Counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Active Requests** | Requests pending or in progress, awaiting your team. |
| **Properties** | Properties you can reach. |
| **Total Rooms** | Rooms across those properties, each with a working QR code. |
| **Menu Orders** | Food and drink orders received. |

All four respect your property access. If you are limited to two hotels, you
see the totals for those two.

## What to do next

| If | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| **Active Requests** is above zero | Open [Guest requests](/dashboard/guest-requests) and work the queue. |
| **Properties** is zero | Add one in [Properties](/properties/properties). |
| **Total Rooms** is zero | Your guests have nothing to scan. Add rooms in the property's [Rooms](/properties/rooms) tab. |

<Callout title="Good to know">

These numbers are counted when the page loads. The red
count beside **Guest Requests** in the sidebar updates on its own — use that
as your live indicator, and reload this page when you want fresh totals.

</Callout>

This page is a summary. Every action happens on the pages it points to.
