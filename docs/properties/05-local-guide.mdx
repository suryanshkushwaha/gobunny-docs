Choose which of your recommendations this property shows, and the order guests
see them in.

Open a property and go to its **Local Guide** tab.

The recommendations themselves are written once for the whole business, in
[Master local guides](/master-setup/local-guides). This page decides
which of them belong to this property.

Guests only see them once **Local Guide** is switched on in
[Property settings](/properties/property-settings).

## Turn a guide on or off

Every guide in your master catalog appears here, grouped by category. Use the
switch on each row.

<Callout title="Good to know">

This is where a group of properties earns its keep. A café
two streets from your city hotel is worth recommending there and useless at
your beach house. Same catalog, different selection.

</Callout>

Switching one off removes it from the guest portal straight away. Switch it
back on when you want it there again.

<Callout title="Good to know">

Unlike services and menu items, a guide has no scheduled
return. It stays off until you turn it back on yourself.

</Callout>

You can also do this from the **Property availability** tab on the master Local
Guides page, which shows every property at once.

## Set the order

Drag a guide to move it within its category. Drag a category header to move the
whole group.

The order saves itself and is the order guests see. Put your strongest
recommendation at the top of each category.

## Categories

Guides are grouped into **Food & Drink**, **Places to Visit**, **Shopping**,
**Essentials**, **Activities**, **Nightlife**, and **Other**.

Categories with nothing switched on are hidden from guests.

## Edit the wording

Select **⋯** → **Edit master guide** to change the name, category, description,
tip, Maps link, or photo.

This changes the guide at **every** property showing it. Only the on/off state
and the order are per property.

## What guests see

On the **Guide** tab of their portal, each guide is a card with its photo,
name, your description, your insider tip, and a button that opens directions.

→ [Guest portal: Guide](/guest-portal/guide)

## What changes reach guests

- Changes reach the guest portal immediately.
- Photos load from the web address you supplied. If one breaks, the card still
  works without it.
- Changes are recorded in [Audit logs](/administration/audit-logs).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| The page says there are no master guides | Add them in [Master local guides](/master-setup/local-guides) first. |
| Guests see no guides | Either nothing is switched on here, or **Local Guide** is off in [Property settings](/properties/property-settings). |
| A guide you added is missing | Reload the page. New master guides appear on every property automatically. |
| A guide is showing that you thought you removed | Check this property's switch, not the master catalog. Deleting is done in [Master local guides](/master-setup/local-guides); switching off is done here. |
| A category is missing for guests | Nothing in it is switched on. Empty categories are hidden. |
