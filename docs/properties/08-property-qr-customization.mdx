Give one property its own QR code style, instead of your business-wide one.

Open a property and go to its **QR Customization** tab.

## Turn on the override

The page starts with one switch: **Enable Custom QR Override**.

| Switch | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Off | This property uses your business QR style. The controls stay hidden. |
| On | The full editor appears, filled in from your business style. |

Turn it off again and the property goes back to your business style.

## The controls

The same as business QR customization. You get the presets — **Classic**,
**Soft Rounded**, **Brand Accent**, and **High Contrast Print** — plus the
advanced controls for dots, corners, background, logo, and scan recovery.

→ [QR customization](/platform-setup/qr-customization) for every
control

## The preview

The preview shows this property's name and a sample room number. If the
property has no rooms yet, you see a note saying to add rooms before printing
room-specific codes.

The preview holds your GoBunny address, not a real room. Real room codes come
from the [Rooms](/properties/rooms) tab.

**Download preview** saves the image, named after the property.

## Save and reset

| Button | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Save** | Applies this style to this property's room QR codes. |
| **Reset** | Clears the override so the property follows your business style again. |

## When to override

- The property has its own sub-brand and colours
- One location prints on darker card and needs the high-contrast preset
- The resort wants a softer style while the city hotel stays sharp

If none of these apply, leave it off and manage QR styling once at business
level.

<Callout title="Good to know">

Changing the style never changes room codes, so codes you
have already printed keep working. Only newly generated images look
different.

</Callout>

Test-print and scan one real room code before running a batch, especially with
coloured dots or a large logo.

The centre logo is always your business logo, from
[Settings](/administration/settings). It cannot be set per property.
