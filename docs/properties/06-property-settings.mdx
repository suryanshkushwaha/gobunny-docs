How one property behaves: its details, which features guests get, whether they
can cancel their own requests, and how to delete the property.

Open a property and go to its **Settings** tab.

## Property details

The left panel summarises the property: name, type, currency, time zone,
contact email and phone, Wi-Fi, checkout time, and address.

Select **Edit** to change any of it.

→ [Properties](/properties/properties#add-a-property) for the full field list

<Callout title="Good to know">

**Local Time Zone** drives menu serving hours, preorder
scheduling, and every cancellation deadline. Get it wrong and menus open at
the wrong time for guests who are standing in the building.

</Callout>

The Wi-Fi and checkout time here are the property **defaults**. Individual
rooms can differ.

→ [Rooms](/properties/rooms)

## Features

Three switches decide what guests get.

| Feature | Gives guests |
| --- | --- |
| **F&B Menu** | The Dining tab, for browsing menus and ordering. |
| **Guest Services** | The Services tab, for requesting housekeeping, maintenance, and the rest. |
| **Local Guide** | The Guide tab, with your recommendations. |

New properties start with **Guest Services** on, and **F&B Menu** and **Local
Guide** off.

Switching one off deletes nothing. The tab stays, showing a closed notice, and
the matching card on the guest's home screen dims. Switch it back on and
everything returns.

Your own dashboard tabs stay available either way, so you can build a menu
before guests can see it.

## Guest cancellation

Once **F&B Menu** and **Guest Services** are on, you decide whether guests can
cancel their own requests, and for how long.

### Orders

1. Switch on **Allow guests to cancel their orders**.
2. Set **Guests can cancel immediate orders within** — minutes from when the
   order was placed. Starts at 5.
3. Set **Guests can cancel scheduled orders up to** — minutes before the
   delivery time they chose. Starts at 120.

### Service requests

1. Switch on **Allow guests to cancel their requests**.
2. Set **Guests can cancel immediate requests within** — minutes from when the
   request was made. Starts at 5.

### How the windows work

- Whole minutes, from 0 to 10,080 (seven days).
- **Immediate** windows count forward from when the request was made.
- **Scheduled** windows count backwards from the delivery time.
- Deadlines use this property's time zone.
- These save on their own. Each change confirms with "Cancellation rules were
  saved for \{property name\}." — there is no Save button.

### What the guest sees

At checkout, a note: "Within 5 mins of placement", or "Up to 120 mins before
your chosen delivery time".

On their Status screen, the exact deadline: "Cancellation allowed until 07:30
PM", changing to "Cancellation window was closed at 07:30 PM" once it passes.
The **Cancel Request** button greys out then.

Guests can only cancel **pending** requests. Once your team starts the work,
they cannot.

→ [Guest portal: Status](/guest-portal/status)

## Danger Zone

**Delete Property** removes the property for good, along with its rooms and
room codes, its menus, its service and guide selections, and all of its setup.

It also removes that property's **guest request history and audit log
entries**. Record anything you need for your accounts before you delete.

Every printed QR code for those rooms stops working. There is no undo.

You confirm in a dialog that names the property.

## What saves, and what is recorded

- Feature switches save immediately, with a confirmation.
- Everything on this page is recorded in
  [Audit logs](/administration/audit-logs), with Wi-Fi passwords hidden.
