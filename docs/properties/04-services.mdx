Choose which services this property offers, set their prices, and set the order
guests see.

Open a property and go to its **Services** tab.

The services themselves are written once for the whole business, in
[Master services](/master-setup/services). This page decides which of
them this property actually offers.

Guests only see them once **Guest Services** is switched on in
[Property settings](/properties/property-settings).

## What you start with

Every service in your master catalog appears here, grouped by category and
**switched off**.

Turn on what this property offers. Nothing reaches a guest until you do.

<Callout title="Good to know">

New services you add to the master catalog later also
arrive here switched off. That is deliberate — a new service goes live when
the property is ready for it, not the moment someone types it in.

</Callout>

## Turn a service on or off

Use the switch on the row.

Switching one off asks when it should come back:

| Choice | Comes back |
| --- | --- |
| **Until Tomorrow** | Tomorrow morning |
| **In 1 Hour** / **In 2 Hours** / **In 4 Hours** | After that long |
| **Until End of Day** | Tonight |
| **Custom Date & Time** | When you choose |
| **Indefinitely** | Only when you turn it back on |

A scheduled return shows as a small amber note on the row. Times follow this
property's time zone.

Use a timed return for the spa that closes at 6pm. Use **Indefinitely** for a
service you have stopped offering.

You can also do this from the **Property availability** tab on the master
Services page, which shows every property at once.

## Set a price

Select **⋯** → **Edit price** on any service.

Leave it blank to offer the service free. The guest portal shows no price for
free services.

Prices are per property, in that property's currency. The same service can cost
one amount at your city hotel and another at the beach house.

## Set the order

Drag a service to move it within its category. Drag a category header to move
the whole group.

The order saves itself and is the order guests see. Put what guests ask for
most at the top.

## Edit the wording

Select **⋯** → **Edit master service** to change the name, category, or
description.

This changes the service at **every** property. Prices and on/off state stay
per property.

## Categories decide who gets the work

| Category | Goes to |
| --- | --- |
| Housekeeping | Housekeeping staff |
| Laundry | Housekeeping staff |
| Maintenance | Maintenance staff |
| Front Desk | Front desk staff |
| Other | Front desk staff |

Owners, admins, property managers, and services managers see everything.

→ [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-and-permissions)

## What guests see

On the **Services** tab of their portal, each service you switched on is a card
with an icon, the name, the description, the price if there is one, and a
**Request** button. Tapping it opens a note field — "two extra blankets,
please" — and submits.

→ [Guest portal: Services](/guest-portal/services)

## What changes reach guests

- Changes reach the guest portal immediately.
- A priced service shows the guest what they will be charged before they
  submit, and the price appears on the request in your dashboard and in
  Telegram.
- GoBunny confirms the name, category, and price when a request arrives, so
  what lands in your queue is always the real thing.
- Guests can cancel their own requests if you allow it in
  [Property settings](/properties/property-settings).
- Changes are recorded in [Audit logs](/administration/audit-logs).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| The page says there are no master services | Add them in [Master services](/master-setup/services) first. |
| A service you added is missing here | Reload the page. New master services appear on every property automatically. |
| Guests see no services | Either nothing is switched on, or **Guest Services** is off in [Property settings](/properties/property-settings). |
| A service you turned off came back | It had a scheduled return time. Turn it off again and choose **Indefinitely**. |
| The wrong team is getting a request | Its category is wrong. Select **⋯** → **Edit master service** and change it. |
