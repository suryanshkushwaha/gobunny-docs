Give one property a look of its own, instead of your business-wide style.

Open a property and go to its **Branding** tab.

Useful when a group runs a beach resort and a city hotel that should not look
the same.

## Turn on the override

The page starts with one switch: **Enable Custom Styles Override**.

| Switch | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Off | This property uses your business branding. The controls stay hidden. |
| On | The full editor appears, filled in from your business branding as a starting point. |

Turn it off again and the property goes back to your business branding.

## The controls

The same as business branding — theme presets, fonts and shapes, welcome
wording, cover image, custom colours, and custom CSS. Only the scope differs.

→ [Branding](/platform-setup/branding) for every control

## The preview

The phone on the right shows **this property's** portal, with its real name,
contact details, currency, and features. On a phone it moves behind a
**Preview** button.

## Save and reset

| Button | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Save** | Applies these styles to this property. |
| **Reset** | Clears the override so the property follows your business branding again. |

An amber **Unsaved changes** marker appears while you have pending edits, and
GoBunny warns you before you leave the page.

## How the look is decided

For this property, GoBunny works down this order:

1. This property's branding, if the override is on
2. Your business branding
3. GoBunny's own defaults

Each setting falls back on its own. Override just the cover image and the
headline, and the property still uses your business colours and fonts.

<Callout title="Good to know">

The pattern that works for a group is to set your house
style once at business level — preset, fonts, corner style — then override
only the cover image and headline per property. Consistent brand, distinct
front page.

</Callout>

## What branding does not cover

- Branding does not change which features guests get. That is
  [Property settings](/properties/property-settings).
- Your logo is shared by every property and is set in
  [Settings](/administration/settings).
- QR code styling is separate, with its own override.
