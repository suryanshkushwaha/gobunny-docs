How your guest portal looks. What you set here applies to every property,
unless a property overrides it.

Go to **Platform Setup → Branding**.

## The layout

Controls on the left, a live preview on the right, and a header at the top with
**Unsaved changes**, **Reset**, and **Save**.

The preview is a real guest portal in a phone frame. Every change shows up
straight away. On a phone it moves behind a **Preview** button.

It is not a still image. The preview comes stocked with sample dishes,
services, and local guide entries, so you can tap through every tab, open an
item, add it to the basket, and place the order. You get the same **Order
Placed!** confirmation a guest gets, and the order appears on the preview's
Status tab.

Nothing you do in there leaves it. No request reaches your queue, no
notification goes out, copying the Wi-Fi password does not touch your
clipboard, and switching the preview to dark mode does not change the theme a
guest would see.

The preview stops short of the parts that need a live request: an order placed
in it stays **Pending**, statuses never move, and **Cancel Request** does
nothing. That is the point at which you are looking at the real portal instead.

<Callout title="Good to know">

The sample content is fixed, and it is not your menu. It is there so you can
judge your styling against a full screen instead of an empty one.

</Callout>

Nothing saves until you select **Save**. GoBunny warns you before you leave
with unsaved work.

## Theme presets

Five ready-made colour schemes, each shown with swatches:

**GoBunny Classic**, **Midnight Luxury**, **Forest Retreat**, **Boutique
Terracotta**, and **Ocean Minimalist**.

<Callout title="Good to know">

This is the normal way to set colours. Pick the preset
closest to your brand and move on — you do not need to touch individual
colour values.

</Callout>

## Typography & Shapes

| Control | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Font Style** | Classic Sans (Modern), Elegant Serif (Luxury), Editorial Sans (Boutique), or Monospace / Tech. |
| **Border Radius** | Sharp Edges, Soft Curves, Standard Radius, Ultra Round, or Pills / Circle. |
| **Border Thickness** | No Borders, Thin Lines, or Thick Borders. |

## Welcome Copy

The two lines across the top of the guest portal.

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Top Line** | The small line above the title. Starts as "Welcome to". Up to 80 characters. Leave it empty to hide it. |
| **Headline Override** | The large line. Leave it empty to use the property's name. Up to 120 characters. |

## Cover Image

**Cover Image URL** — a link to a photo. It fills the banner behind your
welcome wording.

Use a wide landscape image. GoBunny darkens it slightly so the text stays
readable.

If the link breaks, the portal falls back to your background colour rather than
showing a broken image.

## Custom colours

Switch on **Enable Custom Colors** to set every colour the portal uses, split
into **Light Theme Variables** and **Dark Theme Variables**.

Each value is a HEX code such as `#635bff`, with a colour picker beside it.

There is also a dark mode switch. Turn it off and guests only get the light
theme — the toggle disappears from their portal.

Your preset still applies unless custom colours are on, so you can switch this
off at any time and go straight back.

Most businesses never need this.

## Custom CSS

Switch on **Enable Custom CSS** for fine control. Target the portal's element
IDs:

```css
#portal-hero {
  min-height: 280px;
}

#wifi-card {
  border-radius: 20px;
}
```

- Up to 12,000 characters.
- `<script>` and `<style>` tags are rejected.
- What you save is applied as `!important`, so it wins over the built-in
  styles.

<Callout title="Good to know">

This can break your guest portal. Only use it if you are
comfortable with CSS, and check the preview before saving.

</Callout>

## Save and reset

| Button | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Save** | Applies your changes to every property that has not overridden branding. |
| **Reset** | Clears your branding back to GoBunny's defaults. |

While you have unsaved changes, an amber **Unsaved changes** marker appears,
and leaving the page — by link, back button, refresh, or closing the tab —
warns you first.

## Per-property overrides

Any property can break away and use its own look. Open it and go to its
**Branding** tab.

→ [Property branding](/properties/property-branding)

For any property, GoBunny works down this order:

1. That property's branding, if its override is on
2. Your business branding, from this page
3. GoBunny's defaults

Each setting falls back on its own, so a property can override just its cover
image and keep everything else.

## What branding does not cover

- The preview shows the real guest portal, not a mock-up. The styling you see is
  the styling guests get — only the dishes and services inside it are samples.
- Branding is visual only. It does not change which features guests get — that
  is [Property settings](/properties/property-settings).
- Your logo is not set here. It lives in
  [Settings](/administration/settings), and appears on the portal, in
  the dashboard, and optionally in the centre of your QR codes.
