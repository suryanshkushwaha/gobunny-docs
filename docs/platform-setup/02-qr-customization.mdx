How your room QR codes look — dot shapes, colours, corner styles, and whether
your logo sits in the middle.

Go to **Platform Setup → QR Customization**.

What you set here applies to every property, unless a property overrides it.

## The layout

Controls on the left, a live preview of a printable card on the right with a
**Download preview** button, and a header with **Unsaved changes**, **Reset**,
and **Save**.

On a phone the preview moves behind a **Preview** button.

Nothing saves until you select **Save**.

## Presets

Four ready-made styles.

| Preset | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **Classic** | Sharp and scan-safe. The safe default. |
| **Soft Rounded** | Gentle corners and dots. |
| **Brand Accent** | Coloured and polished. |
| **High Contrast Print** | Black on white. Best for paper cards. |

<Callout title="Good to know">

Start with a preset. Every one is tested to scan reliably,
and most businesses never need to go further.

</Callout>

## Advanced controls

Switch on **Enable Custom Advanced Controls** for four more sections. Leave it
off and your preset applies as it is.

### Dots

| Control | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Style** | Square, Rounded, Dots, Classy, Classy Rounded, or Extra Rounded. |
| **Color** | A HEX code, with a colour picker. |

### Corners

| Control | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Outer style** | Square, Dot, Rounded, Extra Rounded, Dots, Classy, or Classy Rounded. |
| **Outer color** | HEX. |
| **Inner style** | Square, Dot, Rounded, Dots, Classy, Classy Rounded, or Extra Rounded. |
| **Inner color** | HEX. |

### Background

| Control | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Color** | HEX. Keep it light — dark dots on a dark background do not scan. |
| **Margin** | Clear space around the code, in pixels. |

### Logo

| Control | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Display business logo in center** | Uses your logo from [Settings](/administration/settings). If you have not uploaded one, an **Add logo** button appears instead. |
| **Logo size** | 0 to 0.5, as a proportion of the code. 0.5 is the largest that stays reliable. |
| **Logo margin** | Clear space around the logo, in pixels. |

Change your logo in Settings and every QR preview, download, and print card
updates on its own. You do not have to redo anything here.

### Scan recovery

**Recovery level** — L, M, Q, or H. Higher levels let a code survive more
damage or something covering part of it.

Keep it at **H** whenever you use a centre logo or softer dot shapes. Both eat
into the readable area.

## Save, reset, and download

| Button | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Save** | Applies the style to every property that has not overridden it. |
| **Reset** | Returns to GoBunny's defaults. |
| **Download preview** | Saves the preview image, for checking print quality. |

## The preview is not a real room code

The preview holds your GoBunny web address, never a real room. It is there to
show you the **style**.

Real room QR codes hold their own room link and are downloaded or printed from
the property's [Rooms](/properties/rooms) tab.

## Test before you print

Before you print a hundred tent cards:

1. Save your style.
2. Open a property's **Rooms** tab and download one real room QR code.
3. Print it at the size you plan to use.
4. Scan it with at least two phones, in the light of the actual room.

Coloured dots, a large logo, a low recovery level, and a small print size are
the usual reasons a code scans on a screen but not on paper.

## Per-property overrides

Any property can use a different style. Open it and go to its **QR
Customization** tab.

→ [Property QR customization](/properties/property-qr-customization)

## What styling does not change

- Changing the style never changes the room code, so codes you have already
  printed keep working. Only newly generated images look different.
- If a value is invalid, the field is highlighted and a message points you to
  it.
