The first screen you reach at GoBunny. Two buttons: sign in, or create a
business.

## Choose your route

| You have | Select |
| --- | --- |
| A GoBunny account | **Sign in** → [Sign in](/before-you-sign-in/sign-in) |
| An invite link from a colleague | Open the link → [Accept a team invite](/before-you-sign-in/accept-team-invite) |
| Neither | **Sign up** → [Sign up](/before-you-sign-in/sign-up) |

If a colleague invited you, use their link. **Sign up** creates a separate new
business instead of adding you to theirs.

## Light and dark

The switcher in the top right changes how GoBunny looks. It is on every screen,
and your choice is remembered on that device.

<Callout title="Good to know">

Your guests never reach this page. Their QR code opens
their room's portal directly, with no app, no account, and no sign-in.

</Callout>

If you are already signed in on this device, opening this page or the sign-in
screen takes you straight to your dashboard.
