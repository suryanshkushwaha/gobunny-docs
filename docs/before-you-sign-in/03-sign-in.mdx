Sign in with the email and password you set at sign-up, or when you accepted a
team invite.

## Sign in

1. Enter your **Email**.
2. Enter your **Password**. Use the eye icon to check what you typed.
3. Select **Sign in**.

You land on the first page your role can reach. For owners, admins, property
managers, and services managers that is the dashboard. For everyone else it is
usually **Guest Requests**.

## Forgotten password

Select **Forgot password?** beside the Password label. GoBunny emails you a
secure link.

→ [Reset password](/before-you-sign-in/reset-password)

## Good to know

- One email address belongs to one GoBunny account. If you work with two
  businesses, you need two email addresses.
- If your access has been removed or made inactive, signing in works but your
  business will not load. Ask an owner or admin to restore it.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Enter a valid email address." | Check for typos. |
| Your details are rejected | Confirm the email, then use **Forgot password?**. |
| You verified your email but still cannot sign in | Wait a few seconds and try again. If it persists, request a password reset. |
| You sign in but see no properties | Your role is limited to specific properties, or your access is inactive. Ask an owner or admin. |
