Join a business someone invited you to.

Open the link they sent. Do not use [Sign up](/before-you-sign-in/sign-up) — that creates a
new business of your own instead of joining theirs.

## Join

1. Open the invite link.
2. Check the heading. It reads **Join \{business name\}** and shows the address
   the invite was sent to.
3. Enter your **First name** and **Last name**.
4. Enter a **Password**:
   - At least 8 characters
   - At least 1 number
   - At least 1 special character
5. Select **Join account**.

## Email verification

If GoBunny asks you to confirm your email, select the button that opens your
inbox, open the GoBunny email, and select the link inside. Then sign in.

Otherwise you go straight into the dashboard.

## What you can reach

Whoever invited you chose three things:

| Setting | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Role** | Which pages and actions you get, from Admin down to Read only. |
| **Property access** | Which properties you can work in. It may be all of them, or a specific few. |
| **Custom role** | An exact list of permissions, shown under the name they chose — for example "Night supervisor". |

Anything your role does not include will not appear in your sidebar.

→ [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-and-permissions)

<Callout title="Good to know">

The email address is fixed by the invite. You cannot join
with a different one on the same link. If that address already has a GoBunny
account, ask for the invite to be sent somewhere else.

</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Invite unavailable" | The invite expired, was already used, or was withdrawn. Ask for a new one. |
| The business name looks wrong | Check you opened the right link, and ask the sender to resend. |
| A page is missing from your sidebar | Your role does not include it. Ask an owner or admin to widen your access. |
| You see fewer properties than expected | Your access is limited to specific properties. Ask an owner or admin. |
