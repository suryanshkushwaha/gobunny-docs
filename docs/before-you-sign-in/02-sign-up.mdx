Create your GoBunny account and your business together, in three steps.

Use this screen only to start a **new business**. If a colleague sent you an
invite link, use [Accept a team invite](/before-you-sign-in/accept-team-invite) instead — one
email address belongs to one account.

## Step 1 — Tell us about you

| Field | Required | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First name | Yes | Up to 80 characters. |
| Last name | Yes | Up to 80 characters. |

Select **Continue**.

## Step 2 — Tell us about your business

| Field | Required | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business logo | No | JPG, JPEG, or PNG, under 200KB. |
| Business name | Yes | 2 to 120 characters. |
| Business tagline | No | Up to 160 characters. |

Select **Continue**, or **Back** to change your name.

### Add your logo

1. Select the upload area.
2. Choose a JPG, JPEG, or PNG under 200KB.
3. Drag and zoom to frame the logo.
4. Choose **Square** or **Circle**.
5. Save.

The shape you pick is used everywhere your logo appears: the sidebar, the guest
portal, and the centre of your QR codes.

<Callout title="Good to know">

You can skip the logo and add it later in
[Settings](/administration/settings). Add it before you print any QR
codes if you want it in the middle of them.

</Callout>

## Step 3 — Secure your account

| Field | Required | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email | Yes | A valid address. This becomes your sign-in. |
| Password | Yes | At least 8 characters, 1 number, and 1 special character. |

A checklist under the password ticks off each rule as you type. Use the eye
icon to reveal what you typed.

Select **Sign up**.

## Email verification

If GoBunny asks you to confirm your email, it shows **Please verify your
account to continue**, along with the address it sent to.

1. Select **Open Gmail**, **Open Outlook**, or **Open Yahoo**. The button
   matches your provider.
2. Open the GoBunny email and select the link inside.
3. Sign in.

Otherwise you go straight to your dashboard.

## What you get

Signing up creates your sign-in, your business, and your **Owner** role. Owner
is the highest level of access, including team management, and cannot be given
to anyone else.

Your dashboard starts empty. Add a property next.

→ [Getting started](/getting-started)

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| A field is highlighted in red | Read the message under it. Each step must be valid before you can continue. |
| "Logo must be smaller than 200KB." | Compress or resize the image. |
| "Logo must be a JPG, JPEG, or PNG file." | Convert the file. SVG and WebP are not accepted. |
| The email is already in use | That address already has a GoBunny account. Sign in, or use [Reset password](/before-you-sign-in/reset-password). |
| No verification email | Check spam. Requesting a password reset sends the link again. |
