Set a new password when you have forgotten yours.

This takes two steps: request a link, then use it. The screen shows whichever
step you are on.

## 1. Request a link

1. Select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen.
2. Enter the email address on your GoBunny account.
3. Select **Send link**.

Check your inbox. Select **Sign in** to go back without sending anything.

## 2. Set your new password

Open the link in the email. GoBunny checks it and shows **Set a new password**.

1. Enter your **New password**:
   - At least 8 characters
   - At least 1 number
   - At least 1 special character
2. Enter it again in **Confirm new password**.
3. Select **Update password**.

You are signed in and taken to your dashboard.

## Changing your password while signed in

You do not have to sign out. Go to
[Settings](/administration/settings), find **Password** under Profile
Settings, and select **Reset Password**. GoBunny emails you the same link.

<Callout title="Good to know">

Being signed in is not enough to change your password —
you always have to open a fresh link from your email. That stops anyone using
an unattended, already-signed-in browser to take over your account.

</Callout>

Each link works once and expires.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "That reset link is no longer valid. Request a new link to continue." | The link expired or was already used. Request another. |
| "Passwords must match." | The two fields differ. Retype both. |
| No email arrives | Check spam. Confirm you typed the address you signed up with. |
